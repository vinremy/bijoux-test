About me

I am a jewelry maker with a strong interest in material, process and form.

I enjoy working with my hands, exploring traditional techniques and finding new ways to combine them with contemporary design.

I am looking for a position as a goldsmith or bench jeweler in Amsterdam, to develop my skills and contribute to a creative studio or workshop.

Skills and interests

  • Sawing, filing, soldering, polishing
  • Stone setting, basic to intermediate
  • Working with silver, gold and other metals
  • Handcraft and traditional techniques
  • Design development and problem solving

From observation to form

Ideas often start with nature, a texture or a feeling. I sketch, explore materials and test different shapes before moving to the final piece.

Two drawings

Every piece is drawn twice. First comes a technical drawing, at the very start: it helps me understand proportions, fit and construction before I touch the metal.

Then a creative drawing gives the piece its identity: its colour, its light, its character.

Gouache and ink: two studies of the same grooved ring.

Materials and techniques

I work with traditional hand tools and techniques, while staying open to new methods and materials. Each step is part of the creative and technical process.

Workshop

My workspace is where ideas become real. I value a clean, functional setup and the quiet focus that comes with working by hand.

Amsterdam

Amsterdam is a city that values craftsmanship, creativity and diversity.

I want to join a studio where I can learn from experienced goldsmiths, work on meaningful projects and be part of a community that shares the same passion for jewelry and design.

My goals

  • Work on unique and meaningful pieces
  • Contribute to a creative studio or atelier in Amsterdam
