Work

One-off pieces in sterling silver, each one designed, sawn, soldered, set and polished at the bench. Twenty-eight of them, from the graduation collection to the latest rings.

Constellations

Graduation collection, École de joaillerie de Montréal, 2023

My work is built on a geometric, systemic aesthetic. For this collection I set myself one constraint: make a necklace and a bracelet with no clasp. The geometry is borrowed from nature, repeated part by part until it becomes a structure that moves.

Couronne Boréale necklace

Through the expansion and retraction of some of its parts, the necklace adjusts to the body. It settles around the neck according to whichever arrangement you choose.

Material
Sterling silver
Fastening
None, the necklace adjusts by movement
Techniques
Sawing, filing, soldering, articulation

Collision Stellaire bracelet

The bracelet fits every type of wrist thanks to a spring system I developed for it. No clasp, no fixed size.

Material
Sterling silver, star sapphire
Fastening
Spring system
Techniques
Sawing, soldering, stone setting
